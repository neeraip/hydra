Hydra

The simulation engines, compiled to WebAssembly and running on this page. Nothing is uploaded — the model is read, solved and reported here.

Drop a model here, or click to choose one.

An EPANET or SWMM .inp file. Drop auxiliary files (climate, hotstart, routing inflows) alongside it and they will be matched by name.

No model to hand? Try one of these:
hydra run …
